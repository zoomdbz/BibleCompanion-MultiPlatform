## Библиография

Это первичные, святоотеческие и научные источники, лежащие в основе учебных примечаний и введений к книгам в этом приложении.

## Первичные древние источники (греческие и римские историки)

- Herodotus. *The Persian Wars (Histories).* Translated by A. D. Godley. 4 vols. Loeb Classical Library 117-120. Cambridge, MA: Harvard University Press, 1920-1925.

- Xenophon. *Anabasis.* Translated by Carleton L. Brownson. Loeb Classical Library 90. Cambridge, MA: Harvard University Press, 1922 (rev. John Dillery, 1998).

- Tacitus. *The Annals.* Translated by John Jackson. Loeb Classical Library 249, 312, 322. Cambridge, MA: Harvard University Press, 1931-1937.

- Pliny the Younger. *Letters, Volume II: Books 8-10; Panegyricus* (including the correspondence with Trajan, Book 10). Translated by Betty Radice. Loeb Classical Library 59. Cambridge, MA: Harvard University Press, 1969.

- Pliny the Elder. *Natural History.* Translated by H. Rackham et al. 10 vols. Loeb Classical Library. Cambridge, MA: Harvard University Press, 1938-1962.

## Иудейские первичные источники

- Josephus. *Jewish Antiquities (Antiquities of the Jews).* Translated by H. St. J. Thackeray, Ralph Marcus, and Allen Wikgren. Loeb Classical Library. Cambridge, MA: Harvard University Press, 1930-1965.

- Josephus. *The Jewish War.* Translated by H. St. J. Thackeray. Loeb Classical Library 203, 487, 210. Cambridge, MA: Harvard University Press, 1927-1928.

- *The Babylonian Talmud*, tractate Bava Batra 14b-15a (on the order and authorship of the biblical books). Standard reference text; see the Sefaria critical and translation edition.

- *The Dead Sea Scrolls.* Critical edition: *Discoveries in the Judaean Desert*, 40 vols. Oxford: Clarendon Press, 1955-2009. Study edition: Florentino Garcia Martinez and Eibert J. C. Tigchelaar, *The Dead Sea Scrolls Study Edition.* Grand Rapids: Eerdmans, 1999.

- *Septuaginta (The Septuagint, LXX).* Edited by Alfred Rahlfs, revised by Robert Hanhart. Editio altera. Stuttgart: Deutsche Bibelgesellschaft, 2006 (original Rahlfs, 1935).

## Святоотеческие и раннецерковные источники

- Eusebius of Caesarea. *The Ecclesiastical History (Historia Ecclesiastica).* Translated by Kirsopp Lake and J. E. L. Oulton. 2 vols. Loeb Classical Library 153, 265. Cambridge, MA: Harvard University Press, 1926-1932.

- Clement of Rome. *First Epistle to the Corinthians (1 Clement).* In *The Apostolic Fathers, Volume I.* Edited and translated by Bart D. Ehrman. Loeb Classical Library 24. Cambridge, MA: Harvard University Press, 2003.

- Tertullian. *The Prescription Against Heretics (De Praescriptione Haereticorum).* Translated by Peter Holmes. In *Ante-Nicene Fathers*, Vol. 3. Edited by Alexander Roberts and James Donaldson. Buffalo: Christian Literature Publishing, 1885.

- Irenaeus. *Against Heresies (Adversus Haereses).* Translated by Alexander Roberts and W. H. Rambaut. In *Ante-Nicene Fathers*, Vol. 1. Edited by Alexander Roberts and James Donaldson. Buffalo: Christian Literature Publishing, 1885.

- Jerome. *On Illustrious Men (De Viris Illustribus).* Translated by Ernest Cushing Richardson. In *Nicene and Post-Nicene Fathers*, Series 2, Vol. 3. Edited by Philip Schaff and Henry Wace. Buffalo: Christian Literature Publishing, 1892.

- Origen. Commentaries and homilies as preserved in Eusebius, *Ecclesiastical History* (especially 6.25, on the four Gospels and the Epistle to the Hebrews). Loeb Classical Library 265.

- Hegesippus. *Memoirs (Hypomnemata)*, surviving in fragments preserved by Eusebius, *Ecclesiastical History* (II.23; III.11, 16, 19, 20, 32; IV.8, 22). Loeb Classical Library 153.

- Polycrates of Ephesus. Letter to Victor of Rome on the Quartodeciman question, preserved in Eusebius, *Ecclesiastical History* V.24. Loeb Classical Library 153.

- Nikephoros Kallistos Xanthopoulos. *Ecclesiastical History (Historia Ecclesiastica).* In J.-P. Migne, ed., *Patrologia Graeca*, vols. 145-147. Paris, 1865. (PG 145 covers books 1-7.)

## Списки канона, прологи и агиографические источники

- *The Muratorian Fragment (Muratorian Canon).* Late-second-century Latin list of New Testament books; edited and translated in Bruce M. Metzger, *The Canon of the New Testament.* Oxford: Clarendon Press, 1987, Appendix IV.

- *The Anti-Marcionite Prologues* (to the Gospels of Mark, Luke, and John). Early Latin gospel prologues; texts collected by Donatien de Bruyne and reprinted in standard apocrypha collections.

- *Martyrdom of Mark* (Greek *Martyrion tou hagiou Markou*) and the later *Acts of Mark* (a post-1000 Byzantine reworking, Cod. Athon. Stauronicetae 18). Cataloged in NASSCAL, *e-Clavis: Christian Apocrypha.*

- *Apostolic History of Abdias (Pseudo-Abdias) / Virtutes Apostolorum.* Medieval Latin collection of apocryphal apostolic acts; cataloged in NASSCAL, *e-Clavis: Christian Apocrypha.*

- *The Ethiopian Synaxarium (Senkessar).* Translated by E. A. Wallis Budge as *The Book of the Saints of the Ethiopian Church.* 4 vols. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 1928.
