# Astronomical Signs

"And God said, 'Let there be lights in the expanse of the heavens to separate the day from the night. And let them be for **signs** and for **seasons** and for days and years'" (Genesis 1:14).

The Hebrew word for "signs" is *otot*: the same word used for the signs God gave Moses (Exodus 4:8–9). The word for "seasons" is *moadim*: the appointed feasts. God designed the heavenly bodies as markers of His prophetic calendar.

## Blood Moons and Lunar Eclipses

A "blood moon" occurs during a total lunar eclipse, when the moon turns red. Joel 2:31 says: "The sun shall be turned to darkness, and the moon to blood, before the great and awesome day of the LORD comes" (cf. Acts 2:20; Revelation 6:12).

### Blood Moon Tetrads and Feast Eclipses: Ancient Events through 2033

A tetrad is four consecutive total lunar eclipses, each separated by about six months. Several historical tetrads have clustered around Passover and Sukkot. Some maxima fall on a feast's eve rather than its first day; local sunset and location matter. The 2024–2027 window adds a distinct pattern: a lunar eclipse on a Purim observance in four consecutive years, with two total blood moons bracketed by penumbral eclipses on Shushan Purim 5784 and Purim Katan 5787. Solar eclipses also coincide with Nisan's beginning, Trumpets, and other new-month observances. The intervening years are not entirely quiet: December 2029 brings a Hanukkah eclipse, June 2030 a new-month alignment in New Zealand, and May 2031 a penumbral eclipse on Pesach Sheni. In 2033, four eclipses cluster around Nisan's beginning, Passover, Trumpets, and Sukkot.

**Ancient chronology:** The ancient dates and the 1493–1494 dates above use the Julian calendar; modern dates use the Gregorian calendar. Astronomical year 0 means 1 BC, not an additional historical year. Josephus records an eclipse before Herod's death but does not date it. The 4 BC and 1 BC entries represent competing identifications, not two eclipses both established as that event. April 7, AD 30 and April 3, AD 33 are the principal proposed Crucifixion dates; the table includes only the actual eclipse on the latter. A lunar eclipse cannot explain the daytime darkness in Matthew 27:45. Ancient feast dates require reconstruction of the observed Jewish calendar; the 1493–1494 Hebrew alignments use a calculated calendar rather than a surviving observation record. No eclipse establishes the exact date of Jesus' birth or proves a prophetic interpretation.

The historical tetrads coincide with major turning points in Jewish history. The 2024–2027 Purim pattern is not a tetrad because two of its four eclipses are penumbral. A separate astronomical tetrad does occur in 2032–2033: April 25 and October 18, 2032, followed by April 14 and October 8, 2033. It differs from the familiar Passover–Sukkot pattern: the first aligns with Pesach Sheni at maximum in Israel, and October 18, 2032 does not align with Passover or Sukkot in the reference calendars. The four selected eclipses in 2033 align with the named feasts, their eves, or Nisan's beginning depending on location. Whether these alignments carry prophetic weight is a matter of interpretation; the astronomical events themselves are documented fact.

## Solar Eclipses

Solar eclipses in Jewish tradition are considered signs to the nations (Sukkah 29a). Several notable eclipses coincide with significant dates:

- **March 20, 2015:** Total solar eclipse on **29 Adar 5775, the eve of Nisan 1** (the biblical new year for months, Exodus 12:2), midway through the 2014–2015 blood moon tetrad. Nisan 1 began at local sunset.
- **September 13, 2015:** Partial solar eclipse on **29 Elul 5775, the eve of Rosh Hashanah**. **1 Tishrei 5776** began at local sunset. Together with the March 20 eclipse, these two solar eclipses fell on the eves of the two most significant months in the Hebrew calendar (Nisan and Tishrei), bracketing the 2014–2015 blood moon tetrad within a single year. Prophecy teachers Mark Biltz and John Hagee treat all six events (four blood moons + two solar eclipses) as one cluster.
- **August 21, 2017:** "Great American Eclipse", first total solar eclipse visible coast-to-coast in the US in 99 years. Along the American path, the Hebrew date was **29 Av 5777**, the eve of Elul. At greatest eclipse, Israel was already past sunset on **30 Av, Rosh Chodesh Elul**. Elul is the 40-day season of repentance leading to Rosh Hashanah; many Messianic teachers noted the timing.
- **April 8, 2024:** Second Great American Eclipse; the two paths (2017 and 2024) form an X over the United States

## The Star of Bethlehem

The Magi saw "his star" (Matthew 2:2). Several astronomical events around 3–2 BC have been proposed:

- **Jupiter-Regulus triple conjunction** (3–2 BC): Jupiter (the "king planet") passed the star Regulus (Latin for "little king") in the constellation Leo (the lion = Judah) three times due to retrograde motion
- **Jupiter-Venus conjunction** (June 17, 2 BC): The two brightest planets merged into a single brilliant "star" visible from Babylon, in the direction of Judea
- **Jupiter's retrograde "stopping"** (December 25, 2 BC): As seen from Jerusalem, Jupiter appeared to stop in the sky directly over Bethlehem to the south, matching Matthew 2:9: "the star... went before them... and came to rest over the place where the child was"

These are not speculative; they are verified by modern astronomical software (e.g., Stellarium). Whether one or a combination of these events was "the star," the alignment with the prophecy of Numbers 24:17 ("a star shall come out of Jacob") is striking.

## Crucifixion Darkness

"From the sixth hour there was darkness over all the land until the ninth hour" (Matthew 27:45; Mark 15:33; Luke 23:44–45).

This was not a solar eclipse; Passover falls at full moon, when solar eclipses are astronomically impossible. The darkness was supernatural. However, the 2nd-century historian Thallus (quoted by Julius Africanus) attempted to explain it as a solar eclipse, inadvertently confirming that the darkness was a widely known historical event.

Amos 8:9: "On that day, declares the Lord GOD, I will make the sun go down at noon and darken the earth in broad daylight."

## The Sign of Revelation 12

"A great sign appeared in heaven: a woman clothed with the sun, with the moon under her feet, and on her head a crown of twelve stars" (Revelation 12:1).

On **September 23, 2017**, the constellation Virgo was "clothed" by the sun with the moon at her feet. Above her head, the nine stars of Leo plus the planets Mercury, Venus, and Mars formed a crown of twelve. Jupiter had been in Virgo's midsection for approximately 42 weeks, the duration of human gestation, due to retrograde motion before "exiting" on September 9, 2017. Jupiter is historically called the "king planet." The 42-week period precisely mirrors the "42 months" of Revelation 13:5 and Revelation 12:6 (1,260 days). Jupiter entered Virgo's womb region on November 20, 2016.

The Hebrew date was **3 Tishrei 5778**, the Fast of Gedaliah, the day after Rosh Hashanah.

This exact full configuration (sun, moon, 12-object crown, and Jupiter's exit after 42 weeks) is astronomically unique in recorded history when all parameters are held together. Whether it fulfills Revelation 12:1–2 is a matter of interpretation, but the astronomical alignment is factual and verifiable using software such as Stellarium.

## Other Notable Celestial Signs

**The Eclipse Comet of 1948 (C/1948 V1)**

On **November 1, 1948**, approximately 1 Kislev 5709, a brilliant comet was discovered during a total solar eclipse. It reached naked-eye visibility with a tail spanning up to 30 degrees of sky and remained visible through December 1948. This was the same year Israel became a nation (May 14, 1948 = 5 Iyyar 5708) after nearly 2,000 years of exile. The coincidence of a naked-eye comet appearing within Israel's first year of modern statehood has not been lost on students of Ezekiel 37 and Isaiah 11.

**The Great Conjunction of December 21, 2020**

On the winter solstice, **December 21, 2020** (6 Tevet 5781), Jupiter and Saturn appeared only 0.1 degrees apart, the closest visible separation since 1623. Major media called it the "Christmas Star" because of its resemblance to descriptions of the Star of Bethlehem. The conjunction appeared in the western sky shortly after sunset, in the direction of Bethlehem as seen from Jerusalem. The Hebrew date falls in Tevet, a month with no feast days, but the visual and symbolic resonance with the Bethlehem star event led many Messianic believers to note it. The two planets will not be this close again until 2080.

**Comet NEOWISE (2020)**

**Comet C/2020 F3 (NEOWISE)** reached perihelion on July 3, 2020 (~11 Tammuz 5780) and closest Earth approach on July 23, 2020 (~2 Av 5780). It was the brightest comet visible to the naked eye from the Northern Hemisphere since Comet Hale-Bopp in 1997. Its estimated orbital period is approximately 4,500 years, leading some to suggest its last appearance was in the era of Abraham or before. The month of Av on the Hebrew calendar is a month of mourning and fasting (Tisha B'Av falls on 9 Av); the comet peaked during this period. Its prophetic significance is debated within the Messianic community.

### 2026: Planetary Signs

The spring and summer of 2026 bring additional alignments:

- **June 9, 2026:** Jupiter-Venus conjunction in the evening sky. Jupiter-Venus conjunctions are the class of event most commonly proposed as candidates for the Star of Bethlehem.
- **June 12, 2026:** Mercury, Venus, and Jupiter visible in alignment in the evening sky.
- **February 20, 2026:** Saturn-Neptune conjunction at 0° Aries, the zodiacal "reset point." This conjunction occurs every 36 years; the exact alignment at the beginning of Aries is rare.

## Genesis 1:14 and the Feasts

The Hebrew word *moadim* connects the celestial bodies directly to God's appointed times:

- Passover always occurs at **full moon** (Nisan 14–15)
- Sukkot always occurs at **full moon** (Tishrei 15)
- The Feast of Trumpets depends on the **new moon** sighting (Tishrei 1)
- The spring equinox determines the start of the biblical year

The heavens are not worshiped (Deuteronomy 4:19), but they are read; they declare the glory of God (Psalm 19:1) and mark His appointed times.

## 2024–2027: A Cluster of Celestial Signs

An unusual concentration of eclipses aligning with Hebrew feast days occurs across 2024–2027. The dates below are verified by NASA's eclipse catalog and the Hebcal Hebrew calendar.

### Lunar Eclipses on Purim: Four Years Running

In four consecutive years, a lunar eclipse falls on a Purim observance. Two penumbral eclipses bracket two total blood moons:

- **March 24–25, 2024:** Penumbral lunar eclipse, 15 Adar II 5784, **Shushan Purim** (the Purim of walled cities, Esther 9:18)
- **March 13–14, 2025:** Total lunar eclipse (blood moon, ~65 minutes of totality), 14 Adar 5785, **Purim**
- **March 2–3, 2026:** Total lunar eclipse (blood moon, ~58 minutes of totality), 14 Adar 5786, **Purim**
- **February 20–21, 2027:** Penumbral lunar eclipse, 14 Adar I 5787, **Purim Katan** (the leap-year Purim of Adar I)

Back-to-back blood moons on the same feast in consecutive years has no modern precedent, and here they arrive framed by penumbral eclipses on Shushan Purim before and Purim Katan after: four years, four Purim observances, four lunar eclipses. Purim is not a Leviticus 23 feast, but it commemorates Israel's deliverance from genocide (Esther 9:20–22). The book of Esther never names God, yet His hand is visible throughout.

### Solar Eclipses

- **April 8, 2024:** Total solar eclipse crossing North America. Along the path, the Hebrew date was **29 Adar II 5784**, before Nisan 1 began at sundown. At maximum, Israel was already past sunset on **1 Nisan, Rosh Chodesh Nisan**.
- **October 2, 2024:** Annular ("ring of fire") solar eclipse on **29 Elul 5784, the eve of Rosh Hashanah**, along the Chilean and Argentine path. At maximum, Israel was already on **1 Tishrei 5785, the Feast of Trumpets (Yom Teruah)** after sunset. This is a calendar alignment, not a claim of eclipse visibility from Israel.
- **February 17, 2026:** Annular ("ring of fire") solar eclipse over Antarctica. Hebrew date: **30 Shevat 5786, Rosh Chodesh Adar**.
- **August 12, 2026:** Total solar eclipse with a diamond-ring phase. The path of totality crosses the Arctic, Greenland, Iceland, and Spain; North Africa sees only a partial eclipse. Along the Iberian path, the Hebrew date is **29 Av 5786**, Elul eve; at maximum, Israel is already past sunset on **30 Av, Rosh Chodesh Elul**. The diamond ring is a brief phase at the start or end of totality, not a separate eclipse type.

**Solar Storms Before the 2026 Purim Blood Moon**

On **February 1–5, 2026**, active region AR4366, a sunspot group 14 times wider than Earth, erupted with six X-class solar flares in four days, including two X8.1-class events among the strongest of Solar Cycle 25. A coronal mass ejection struck Earth on February 5, triggering geomagnetic storms and widespread aurora. This occurred approximately 25 days before the March 3 Purim blood moon.

### Note

The four Purim-observance eclipses in 2024–2027 do not form an astronomical tetrad: two are penumbral, and other eclipses fall between them. What this period presents is a distinct pattern: a solar eclipse on Rosh Hashanah's eve along its American path while Israel is already on Trumpets, a lunar eclipse on a Purim observance in four consecutive years, two of them blood moons, and an extraordinary solar storm preceding the second blood moon.

Luke 21:25, *"There will be signs in sun and moon and stars."* These alignments are documented fact. Their ultimate meaning belongs to God alone.
