# Astronomical Signs

"And God said, 'Let there be lights in the expanse of the heavens to separate the day from the night. And let them be for **signs** and for **seasons** and for days and years'" (Genesis 1:14).

The Hebrew word for "signs" is *otot*: the same word used for the signs God gave Moses (Exodus 4:8–9). The word for "seasons" is *moadim*: the appointed feasts. God designed the heavenly bodies as markers of His prophetic calendar.

---

## Blood Moons and Lunar Eclipses

A "blood moon" occurs during a total lunar eclipse, when the moon turns red. Joel 2:31 says: "The sun shall be turned to darkness, and the moon to blood, before the great and awesome day of the LORD comes" (cf. Acts 2:20; Revelation 6:12).

### Historical Blood Moon Tetrads on Jewish Feasts

A tetrad is four consecutive total lunar eclipses, each separated by about six months. Remarkably, several tetrads have fallen exactly on Passover and Sukkot (Tabernacles):

| Year | Events | Passover Eclipse | Sukkot Eclipse |
|------|--------|-----------------|----------------|
| 1493–1494 | Spanish Inquisition / expulsion of Jews (1492); Columbus reaches the Americas | Passover 1493, 1494 | Sukkot 1493, 1494 |
| 1949–1950 | State of Israel reborn (May 14, 1948) | Passover 1949, 1950 | Sukkot 1949, 1950 |
| 1967–1968 | Six-Day War; Jerusalem reunified under Israel | Passover 1967, 1968 | Sukkot 1967, 1968 |
| 2014–2015 | Significance debated | Passover 2014, 2015 | Sukkot 2014, 2015 |

Not every tetrad aligns with Jewish feasts; but those that do have coincided with major events in Jewish history. The pattern is notable even if one is cautious about reading too much into it.

## Solar Eclipses

Solar eclipses in Jewish tradition are considered signs to the nations (Sukkah 29a). Several notable eclipses coincide with significant dates:

- **March 20, 2015:** Total solar eclipse on Nisan 1 (biblical new year), midway through the 2014–2015 blood moon tetrad
- **August 21, 2017:** "Great American Eclipse," the first total solar eclipse visible coast-to-coast in the US in 99 years
- **April 8, 2024:** Second Great American Eclipse; the two paths form an X over the United States

## The Star of Bethlehem

The Magi saw "his star" (Matthew 2:2). Several astronomical events around 3–2 BC have been proposed:

- **Jupiter-Regulus triple conjunction** (3–2 BC): Jupiter (the "king planet") passed the star Regulus (Latin for "little king") in the constellation Leo (the lion = Judah) three times due to retrograde motion
- **Jupiter-Venus conjunction** (June 17, 2 BC): The two brightest planets merged into a single brilliant "star" visible from Babylon, in the direction of Judea
- **Jupiter's retrograde "stopping"** (December 25, 2 BC): As seen from Jerusalem, Jupiter appeared to stop in the sky directly over Bethlehem to the south, matching Matthew 2:9: "the star... went before them... and came to rest over the place where the child was"

These are not speculative; they are verified by modern astronomical software (e.g., Stellarium). Whether one or a combination of these events was "the star," the alignment with the prophecy of Numbers 24:17 ("a star shall come out of Jacob") is striking.

## Crucifixion Darkness

"From the sixth hour there was darkness over all the land until the ninth hour" (Matthew 27:45; Mark 15:33; Luke 23:44–45).

This was not a solar eclipse; Passover falls at full moon, when solar eclipses are astronomically impossible. The darkness was supernatural. However, the 2nd-century historian Thallus (quoted by Julius Africanus) attempted to explain it as a solar eclipse, inadvertently confirming that the darkness was a widely known historical event.

Amos 8:9: "On that day, declares the Lord GOD, I will make the sun go down at noon and darken the earth in broad daylight."

## The Sign of Revelation 12

"A great sign appeared in heaven: a woman clothed with the sun, with the moon under her feet, and on her head a crown of twelve stars" (Revelation 12:1).

On **September 23, 2017**, the constellation Virgo was "clothed" by the sun with the moon at her feet. Above her head, the nine stars of Leo plus the planets Mercury, Venus, and Mars formed a crown of twelve. Jupiter had been in Virgo's midsection for approximately nine months due to its retrograde motion before "exiting," often described as a birth metaphor.

This exact configuration occurs very rarely. Whether it fulfills Revelation 12 is a matter of interpretation, but the astronomical alignment is factual and verifiable.

## Genesis 1:14 and the Feasts

The Hebrew word *moadim* connects the celestial bodies directly to God's appointed times:

- Passover always occurs at **full moon** (Nisan 14–15)
- Sukkot always occurs at **full moon** (Tishrei 15)
- The Feast of Trumpets depends on the **new moon** sighting (Tishrei 1)
- The spring equinox determines the start of the biblical year

The heavens are not worshiped (Deuteronomy 4:19), but they are read; they declare the glory of God (Psalm 19:1) and mark His appointed times.
