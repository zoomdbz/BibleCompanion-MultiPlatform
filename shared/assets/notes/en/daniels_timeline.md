# Daniel's Timeline

The book of Daniel contains some of the most precise prophetic timelines in all of Scripture. Written during the Babylonian exile (6th century BC), these prophecies map out world empires and the coming of the Messiah with remarkable specificity.

---

## The Statue: Daniel 2

Nebuchadnezzar dreamed of a great statue made of four materials. Daniel interpreted each as a successive world empire:

| Part | Material | Empire | Timeframe |
|------|----------|--------|-----------|
| Head | Gold | Babylon | 605–539 BC |
| Chest & Arms | Silver | Medo-Persia | 539–331 BC |
| Belly & Thighs | Bronze | Greece | 331–168 BC |
| Legs | Iron | Rome | 168 BC – 476 AD |
| Feet | Iron & Clay | Divided kingdom | Future? |

A stone "cut without hands" strikes the statue and becomes a great mountain filling the earth (Daniel 2:34–35, 44–45). This is the kingdom of God, not built by human power, crushing and replacing all earthly empires.

- Daniel 2:44: "The God of heaven will set up a kingdom that shall never be destroyed."
- See also: Luke 20:18; 1 Corinthians 15:24–25

---

## The Four Beasts: Daniel 7

Daniel's own vision parallels the statue but adds detail:

- **Lion with eagle's wings:** Babylon (Daniel 7:4)
- **Bear raised on one side:** Medo-Persia (Daniel 7:5). Three ribs = Lydia, Babylon, Egypt.
- **Leopard with four wings and four heads:** Greece under Alexander, then divided into four kingdoms (Daniel 7:6; 8:21–22): Ptolemaic Egypt, Seleucid Syria, Antigonid Macedonia, Attalid Pergamon.
- **Terrifying beast with iron teeth:** Rome (Daniel 7:7). Ten horns = future division.

The vision climaxes with the "Son of Man" coming on the clouds of heaven to receive an everlasting dominion (Daniel 7:13–14); the title Jesus used for Himself most frequently (Matthew 26:64; Mark 14:62).

## The 70 Weeks: Daniel 9:24–27

This is the most precise messianic timeline in the Bible.

The angel Gabriel told Daniel: **"Seventy weeks are decreed about your people and your holy city"** (Daniel 9:24). The Hebrew word *shabuim* means "sevens"; seventy sets of seven years = **490 years**.

**The Decree**
"From the going out of the word to restore and build Jerusalem": this is the decree of Artaxerxes to Nehemiah in **445 BC** (Nehemiah 2:1–8).

### The Timeline

| Period | Duration | Calculation |
|--------|----------|-------------|
| 7 weeks | 49 years | Rebuilding of Jerusalem (445–396 BC) |
| 62 weeks | 434 years | Until "an anointed one" is cut off |
| Total: 69 weeks | 483 years | 445 BC + 483 prophetic years (360-day) |

Using the prophetic year of 360 days:
- 483 × 360 = 173,880 days
- From Nisan 1, 445 BC → arrives at approximately **Nisan 10, 32 AD**
- Nisan 10 is the day the Passover lamb was selected; the very day Jesus entered Jerusalem on a donkey (Luke 19:28–40; cf. Exodus 12:3).

"After the sixty-two weeks, an anointed one shall be **cut off** and shall have nothing" (Daniel 9:26): the crucifixion.

"And the people of the prince who is to come shall **destroy the city and the sanctuary**" (Daniel 9:26): fulfilled in **70 AD** when Rome destroyed Jerusalem and the Temple.

### The 70th Week
The final "week" (7 years) is separated from the first 69. Interpretations vary:
- Some see it as fulfilled in the destruction of Jerusalem (70 AD)
- Others see it as a future tribulation period (Revelation 6–19)
- The text says the "prince who is to come" will make a covenant for one week and break it at the midpoint (Daniel 9:27; cf. Matthew 24:15; 2 Thessalonians 2:3–4)

## The Ram and the Goat: Daniel 8

- **Ram with two horns:** Medo-Persia (Daniel 8:20). The higher horn (Persia) rose second.
- **Male goat with a great horn:** Greece under Alexander (Daniel 8:21). The horn broken = Alexander's death at 32.
- **Four horns:** The four successor kingdoms (Daniel 8:22).
- **Little horn:** Antiochus IV Epiphanes, who desecrated the Temple in 167 BC (Daniel 8:9–14; 11:31), a type of the future Antichrist.

---

### Key Takeaway

Daniel's prophecies are not vague. They name specific empires in sequence, predict the precise timing of the Messiah's arrival, foretell the destruction of the Second Temple, and outline the pattern of the end times. Jesus Himself pointed back to Daniel: "When you see the abomination of desolation spoken of by the prophet Daniel, standing in the holy place (let the reader understand)" (Matthew 24:15).

- Daniel 2:21: "He changes times and seasons; he removes kings and sets up kings."
- Daniel 12:4: "Shut up the words and seal the book, until the time of the end."
