# Messianic Prophecy Map

The Hebrew Scriptures contain over 300 prophecies pointing to the Messiah. Below are the major ones with their Old Testament source and New Testament fulfillment. Every reference is exact; read them for yourself.

---

## Birth and Origin

- **Born of a virgin:** Isaiah 7:14 → Matthew 1:22–23
- **Born in Bethlehem:** Micah 5:2 → Matthew 2:1–6
- **From the tribe of Judah:** Genesis 49:10 → Matthew 1:2–3; Hebrews 7:14
- **From the line of David:** 2 Samuel 7:12–13; Isaiah 11:1 → Matthew 1:1; Romans 1:3
- **Called out of Egypt:** Hosea 11:1 → Matthew 2:14–15
- **Preceded by a messenger:** Isaiah 40:3; Malachi 3:1 → Matthew 3:1–3; Mark 1:2–4

## Ministry

- **Anointed by the Spirit:** Isaiah 61:1–2 → Luke 4:18–21
- **Ministry in Galilee:** Isaiah 9:1–2 → Matthew 4:13–16
- **A prophet like Moses:** Deuteronomy 18:15–19 → Acts 3:22–23; John 6:14
- **Teaching in parables:** Psalm 78:2 → Matthew 13:34–35
- **Healing the blind, deaf, lame:** Isaiah 35:5–6 → Matthew 11:4–6; John 9:1–7
- **Entering Jerusalem on a donkey:** Zechariah 9:9 → Matthew 21:1–9; John 12:14–16
- **A stone of stumbling:** Isaiah 8:14; Psalm 118:22 → Romans 9:32–33; 1 Peter 2:6–8

## Betrayal and Trial

- **Betrayed by a friend:** Psalm 41:9 → John 13:18–21
- **Sold for thirty pieces of silver:** Zechariah 11:12–13 → Matthew 26:14–16; 27:3–10
- **Silent before accusers:** Isaiah 53:7 → Matthew 27:12–14; Mark 14:60–61
- **Forsaken by disciples:** Zechariah 13:7 → Matthew 26:31, 56
- **Accused by false witnesses:** Psalm 35:11 → Matthew 26:59–61

## Suffering and Crucifixion

- **Hands and feet pierced:** Psalm 22:16 → John 20:25–27; Luke 24:39–40
- **Crucified with transgressors:** Isaiah 53:12 → Mark 15:27–28; Luke 23:33
- **Mocked and ridiculed:** Psalm 22:7–8 → Matthew 27:39–44; Luke 23:35
- **Given vinegar to drink:** Psalm 69:21 → Matthew 27:34, 48; John 19:28–30
- **Garments divided, lots cast:** Psalm 22:18 → John 19:23–24
- **No bones broken:** Psalm 34:20; Exodus 12:46 → John 19:33–36
- **Side pierced:** Zechariah 12:10 → John 19:34, 37
- **Darkness over the land:** Amos 8:9 → Matthew 27:45
- **"My God, my God, why have you forsaken me?":** Psalm 22:1 → Matthew 27:46

## Death and Burial

- **Died as a substitutionary sacrifice:** Isaiah 53:4–6, 10–12 → Romans 5:6–8; 2 Corinthians 5:21
- **Buried in a rich man's tomb:** Isaiah 53:9 → Matthew 27:57–60

## Resurrection and Exaltation

- **Not abandoned to the grave:** Psalm 16:10 → Acts 2:27–31; 13:35–37
- **Raised from the dead:** Psalm 16:10; Isaiah 53:10–11 → Matthew 28:5–6; Acts 2:32
- **Ascended to the right hand of God:** Psalm 110:1 → Acts 2:33–35; Hebrews 1:3
- **A priest forever after the order of Melchizedek:** Psalm 110:4 → Hebrews 5:5–6; 6:20; 7:17

## The Suffering Servant: Isaiah 53

Isaiah 53 is the single most detailed messianic prophecy. Written approximately 700 years before the crucifixion, it describes:
- A man despised and rejected (Isaiah 53:3) → John 1:10–11
- Bearing our griefs and sorrows (Isaiah 53:4) → Matthew 8:17
- Wounded for our transgressions (Isaiah 53:5) → Romans 4:25
- Like a lamb led to slaughter, silent (Isaiah 53:7) → Acts 8:32–35
- His grave with the wicked, tomb with the rich (Isaiah 53:9) → Matthew 27:38, 57–60
- He will see his offspring and prolong his days (Isaiah 53:10) → Resurrection

---

Every prophecy points to Jesus of Nazareth. The mathematical probability of one person fulfilling even eight of these prophecies by chance has been calculated at 1 in 10^17 (Peter Stoner, *Science Speaks*). He fulfilled them all.
